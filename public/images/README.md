Authentic East African features and natural skin texture, realistic hands, credible everyday Kenyan professional setting, warm dappled daylight, lush green garden trees and softly blurred foliage behind. Compose all four people and laptop in the LOWER HALF of the frame, group slightly LEFT OF CENTER, upper half mostly softly out-of-focus bright green foliage with sun flare and room for website copy. Waist-up view, candid collaboration, tasteful realistic commercial photography, 50mm lens. No UI, no graphics, no lettering, no watermark, no exaggerated stock-photo gestures.
